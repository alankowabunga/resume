AI-Assisted Development

Most of this year's code was written by AI agents. My job is to set direction and turn every mistake into the next rule.

  • Humans keep the veto: nothing touching the database, money flow or core logic starts until I approve it explicitly. Silence is not consent.
  • Mistakes become process: role definitions, review checklists and automated gates are all version-controlled — diffable and revertible like any other code. Sixty-odd lessons recorded, each with why it matters and how to apply it.
  • Critical changes get strict review: the most recent core change shipped only after all 54 injected defects were caught by tests.
